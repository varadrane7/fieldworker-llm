A. Customer Information and Guardianship
- Put the customer's own demographic and contact information only in customer_information.
- Capture the customer email from the H/W/C/E fields after customer's information.
- The customer cannot be its own guardian, so do not put under guardianship_contacts.

B. ASO / MCO / Private Insurance
- These are three separate page-1 sections and must not be merged.
- If a section is N/A, keep its object with empty values.
- United Health Care belongs to managed_care_organization.
- Aetna belongs to private_insurance.

C. Diagnosis
- Extract each diagnosis correctly and put them into diagnosis_information.diagnoses. 

D. Service Authorizations
- Each service block becomes one service_authorizations item.
- associated_goals must include both outcome_number and outcome_description.
- Keep outcome labels in the form "Outcome 1", "Outcome 2", etc.

E. Customer Needs
- There are two main section containers:
  1. Health and Nutrition Needs
  2. Safety and Support Needs
- These are section headers only. Do not use them as category_name values.

Health and Nutrition Needs hierarchy:
- Categories include: Allergies, Dietary, Health Hazards, Self-Care.
- Under Allergies, subcategories include Environmental and Medication - TRILEPTAL.
- Under Health Hazards, subcategories include Other and Seizures.
- Under Self-Care, subcategories include Adjusting water temperature, Blowing nose, Chewing & swallowing, Dressing, Drinking on own, Feeding self, Other, Toileting - bladder, Toileting - bowel, Using microwave, Using stove, and Washing hands.

Safety and Support Needs hierarchy:
- Categories include: Behavior, Communication, Religious/Cultural, Support Settings, Mobility.
- Under Behavior, subcategories include Fears/Phobias and Other.
- Under Communication, subcategory includes Non-verbal.
- Under Religious/Cultural, subcategory includes Religious.
- Under Support Settings, subcategories include Community and Sounds.
- Under Mobility, subcategories include Other, Home, and Work.

General rules:
- Do not promote a subcategory into a category.
- If text continues onto the next page under the same subcategory, continue the same need_description.
- The description text of Safety and Support Needs category/subcategory must be added into the need_description of the previous category/subcategory.
- Remove bullet symbols from final text, but preserve separation with newline characters.

F. Text formatting
- For multi-bullet content inside one field, separate bullet items with newline characters.
- Remove bullet symbols from the final text.
